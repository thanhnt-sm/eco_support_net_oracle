# Fast burn

Confirm the SLO panel and valid event count, identify operation and deployment, inspect a representative trace and correlated logs by `trace_id`, then dependency spans and profiler only for the matching service/time range. Mitigate with rollback, traffic protection or dependency failover; verify recovery on the SLI. Preserve evidence and escalate to service owner. If Collector is degraded, business traffic must continue and dropped telemetry must be recorded.
